King’s College · Cambridge

Field notes

In 1936, Alan Turing described a machine able to simulate other computing machines by reading their encoded instructions.

This imagined study and its brass instrument give that idea a physical form. The chapel view was composed for the scene. King’s College records describe his fellowship rooms at the top of Bodley’s building with a view along the River Cam.
